Hi, I'm Lucy.

I'm a creative portrait photographer based in Portland, Oregon. I love how photography brings me into the present moment, and lets me show people how beautiful they are. There's something so empowering about being photographed, knowing that you are worthy of being captured and remembered just as you are.

I'm also a mom of 2 young boys, a very lucky wife, a software engineer, a runner, a reader, and generally a people person! I'm excited to get to know you and make some special memories together.
